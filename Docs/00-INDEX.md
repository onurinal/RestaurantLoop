## Status Vocabulary

- `Open`: known but unresolved.
- `Blocked`: cannot safely proceed without evidence or approval.
- `Proposed`: concrete option awaiting approval.
- `Approved`: explicitly accepted and safe to implement.
- `Verified`: implemented and tested against its acceptance criteria.
